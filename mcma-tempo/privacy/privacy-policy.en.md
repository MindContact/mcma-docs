# Privacy Policy — Tempo

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

Tempo shows the time and the weather forecast. It does that, and collects as
little as possible to do it.

Data controller: MindContact — privacy@mindcontact.net

## In short

* Tempo does **not** create accounts, does **not** ask you to sign up, and does
  **not** collect your name, email or contacts.
* Device location is **optional**: it is used only if you pick "this device" as
  a place, and places can be searched by name without ever granting it.
* The places you save and the last forecast received stay **on the phone**. We
  have no server to keep them on.
* Two things leave the device: the coordinates of the place you want the
  forecast for, sent to Open-Meteo, and whatever Google AdMob collects to show
  ads.

## Permissions, and what they are for

| Permission | Why | What leaves the device |
|---|---|---|
| Internet | ask Open-Meteo for the forecast and show ads | see the two sections below |
| Network state | know whether there is a connection before asking, and show the last reading instead of an error | nothing |
| Approximate location | optional: only if you pick "this device" as a place | the coordinates, rounded, go to Open-Meteo with the forecast request |

Location is **approximate** by choice: a weather forecast does not tell one
street from another, and asking for precise location would be asking for more
than is used.

## Weather forecasts

Forecasts come from **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com), a public service that needs
no key and no account.

* Each refresh sends the **coordinates of the place** you want the forecast for
  — the one you searched by name, or the device's approximate position if you
  chose it — along with the IP address, which is unavoidable in any network
  request.
* No identifier of yours or of the phone is sent: the request carries nothing
  that ties those coordinates to a person.
* When you search a town by name, the text you type is sent to the same
  provider's geocoding service to turn it into coordinates.
* Open-Meteo's terms: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

The answer is kept on the phone so it can be shown without a connection, and is
replaced by the next one.

## Advertising

Tempo shows ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and process:

* the device advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
* the IP address and technical information about the device;
* ad interaction data.

Google processes this data as an independent controller. Policy and options:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can limit personalised ads from the system settings:
**Settings › Privacy › Ads**.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent is
requested through Google's message (Consent Management Platform) before any
personalised ad is shown. You can refuse and keep using the app: you will see
non-personalised ads. Where the law requires it, the **"Ad privacy options"**
entry in the app menu reopens that choice at any time.

## Data collected by us

None. Tempo has no backend, sends no telemetry and uses no analytics. The only
things stored are your preferences — widget typeface and colour, clock format,
units, which app a tap opens — the places you added and the last forecast
received. They stay on the phone and are never transmitted.

## Children

Tempo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from children.

## Your rights

Since we process no personal data, we have nothing to export or delete. For the
data collected by Google AdMob, rights are exercised towards Google under its
policy. Uninstalling the app stops all collection and removes the places and
preferences stored on the phone.

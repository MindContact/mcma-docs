Privacy Policy — Frame

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Frame does one thing, and collects as little as possible to do it.

Data controller: MindContact — privacy@mindcontact.net

In short

  • Frame creates no account, asks for no sign-up, and collects no name, email or contacts.
  • Your photographs stay on the device: Frame reads them to show them and uploads them nowhere.
  • We run no servers: no app data is ever sent to us.
  • The only data leaving your device is what Google AdMob collects to serve ads.

Permissions and what they are for

PermissionWhyWhat leaves the device
Photos and images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)it is the job of the app: reading the photographs on the phone and laying them out in timenothing
Internetadssee “Advertising”

Frame does not ask for ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: the location recorded inside a photograph is never read.

On Android 14 and later you can grant access to selected photographs only: Frame works on those and leaves you a way to pick more.

Your photographs

Frame reads the photographs from the system gallery to show them ordered in time. Neither the photographs nor their thumbnails leave the device: there is no Frame server to send them to, they are copied into no service and they are never used for advertising. Thumbnails live in the app's memory while the app is open and are gone when you close it.

Frame does not edit, move or delete your photographs.

When you share a photograph you pick the destination app yourself and the photograph goes there: what that app does with it follows its policy, not this one.

Advertising

Frame shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process:

  • the device advertising identifier (Advertising ID / IDFA);
  • the IP address and technical device information;
  • ad interaction data.

Google processes this data as an independent controller. Policy and options: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can limit personalised ads from your system settings:

  • Android — Settings › Privacy › Ads
  • iOS — Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message appears on first launch; until you answer it, the app requests no ads at all.

Data that stays on the device

App preferences — in practice, which folders the roll is made of — are stored on the device only and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

With no account and no backend, we hold no data about you and cannot identify you. For data processed by Google AdMob, exercise your rights directly with Google, using the contacts in its own policy.

Any question: privacy@mindcontact.net

Changes

Any change to this policy updates the date at the top. Previous versions remain in the history of the public repository mcma-docs.

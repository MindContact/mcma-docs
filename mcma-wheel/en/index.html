Wheel

Write the names, tap the wheel, and let it decide.

ItalianoEnglish

Who does the dishes, who goes first, who gets the book. It opens with the wheel already there, with something written on it: one tap and it turns, it brakes slowly, and the last half turn takes as long as the first three — because that is the moment anyone watches a wheel for.

What it does

  • Up to 24 entries, each with its own colour.
  • Tap the wheel, or the button: either one spins it.
  • "Take the winner off the wheel", for turns without repeats down to the last name.
  • A tick under the finger on the last turn, when every notch might be the one.
  • It remembers the names: you write them once.
  • In English and Italian, following the phone's language.

It is fair, and you can check

The name is drawn before the wheel moves, and the spin is then built to land on it. That is the right way round: if the outcome fell out of rounding in the friction, the sectors a millionth of a degree wider would come up more often than the ones beside them — barely, but systematically. Here the draw is uniform by construction, and the repository holds a test that spins a seven-name wheel four thousand two hundred times and checks that every name comes up as often as every other.

It is a promise that can be checked, which is why it is written down.

Your data stays yours

The names you write stay on the phone and go nowhere: no account, no sign-up, no server of ours. The app asks for no permissions at all — no location, no contacts, no camera — and needs the network only for the ads.

How it is paid for

One banner anchored at the bottom, and that is all: no ads between spins, no subscription, no extra entries to buy.

# Privacy Policy — Biorhythm

*Last updated: 4 September 2026*

Data controller: MindContact — mindcontact.apps@gmail.com

Biorhythm draws three curves from one date. That date never leaves your phone.

## In short

- Biorhythm creates no account, asks for no sign-up, collects no name, email or
  contacts.
- Your date of birth is stored **on the device only** and is sent neither to us
  nor to anyone else. We run no servers.
- The only data leaving the device is what Google AdMob collects to serve ads.
- No health data is collected: a biorhythm is a calendar curiosity, not a
  measurement of your body.

## Permissions and what they are for

| Permission | Why | What leaves the device |
|---|---|---|
| Internet | ads and the consent message | see “Advertising” |

Biorhythm asks for no camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, files
or notifications.

## Your date of birth

- You are asked once, because it is the only input the cycles are derived from.
- It is kept in the app's local preferences, on your device. It is not
  transmitted and is tied to no identifier.
- You can change it from the settings; uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Someone else's date of birth

The “Affinity” feature lets you enter a second date of birth. Everything above
applies: it stays on the device, it is not transmitted, and one tap removes it.
That date belongs to another person: enter it only if you have a personal
relationship with them and consider it reasonable to do so.

## Advertising

Biorhythm shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising
identifier, the IP address, technical device information and ad interaction
data. Google processes it as an independent controller:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

Your date of birth is **not** passed to the ad network.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message appears on first
launch; until it is answered the app requests no ads.

## Not a medical tool

The 23, 28 and 33-day cycles are a nineteenth-century theory with no scientific
support. Biorhythm draws them because they are pleasant to watch, not because
they measure anything.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

With no account and no backend we hold no data about you: there is nothing to
ask us to delete, because nothing ever reached us. For data processed by Google
AdMob, exercise your rights with Google.

Questions: mindcontact.apps@gmail.com

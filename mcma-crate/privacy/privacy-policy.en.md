# Privacy Policy — Crate

*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

Data controller: MindContact — privacy@mindcontact.net

## In short

- Crate creates no account, asks for no sign-up, collects no name, email
  or contacts.
- We run no servers: no app data is ever sent to us.
- The only data leaving the device is what Google AdMob collects.

## Permissions and what they are for

| Permission | Why | What leaves the device |
|---|---|---|
| Internet | ads | see “Advertising” |
| Vibration | the light knock when a crate reaches its target | nothing |

## Advertising

Crate shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising
identifier, the IP address, technical device information and ad interaction
data. Google processes it as an independent controller:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message appears on first
launch; until it is answered the app requests no ads.

## Data that stays on the device

The level you have reached, your best moves for every level and the vibration
switch are stored on the device only and are removed on uninstall. There is
no online leaderboard.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

With no account and no backend we hold no data about you. For data processed by
Google AdMob, exercise your rights with Google.

Questions: privacy@mindcontact.net
